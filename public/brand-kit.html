03 Logo, never

  • Stretch or condense it
  • Recolour it beyond navy, cream, black or white
  • Outline it or add a drop shadow
  • Remove the registered symbol
  • Pull out one letter, such as a lone Z in a circle
  • Add taglines or store names to the lockup
  • Place it over busy imagery or motion

08 Type

Comfort starts with great design.

Bold, loosely tracked label

Body copy is set in a plain, legible weight so it stays easy to read on a showroom tablet.

Shown in Georgia and Arial, the named system fallbacks. Bookmania (Semibold headlines) and Gotham (Bold labels, Book body) are licensed and not bundled.

08

09 Type rules

  • Sentence case for full thoughts, title case for short thematic lines
  • Full stop only on full sentences
  • Digital system fonts in email
  • Flourished Bookmania glyphs
  • Bookmania in all caps
  • Weights outside the approved set
  • Italics where grammar doesn't call for them
