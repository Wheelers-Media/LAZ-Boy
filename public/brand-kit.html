03 · Logo rules

  • Minimum width 100 px / 1.39 in / 35.3 mm
  • Clear space = the height of the “o”, on every side
  • Black logo on light, white logo on dark, only when colour isn't available
  • Stretch or condense it
  • Use any colour but navy, cream, black or white
  • Outline it, or add a drop shadow
  • Remove the registered trademark symbol

13 · Type, print · icons

Print set (signage, banners, ads): Bookmania Semibold / Regular / Semibold Italic for headlines and call-outs; Gotham Book, Bold (+100) and Narrow Book for subhead, label, body and legal. Licensed; never use Bookmania's flourished glyphs. Not used in this app (p.14).

Icons come from Iconoir: optical size 45, stroke weight 1.3, and only in palette colours (p.15). The app uses lucide-react at the same 1.3 stroke, in palette colours.
