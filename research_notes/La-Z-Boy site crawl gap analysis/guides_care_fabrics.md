"Sueded Micro Denier" = micro denier yarn brushed twice (before dyeing, then a pin-brush "suede" process), dyed and sheared, then a cleanability finish; tight weave keeps dirt/dust/food particles out. "Padded Sueded Micro Denier" adds a padding layer between face and backing for dimension and softness. "Recycled Leather" = composite of recycled leather scraps with a cellular polyurethane face; after-treatment NOT recommended.
