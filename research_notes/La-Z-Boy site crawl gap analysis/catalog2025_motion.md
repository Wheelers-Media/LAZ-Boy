# La-Z-Boy Motion Collection Summer/Fall 2025 catalog vs the LAZ-Boy app (2026 editions)

Source: https://view.publitas.com/la-z-boy/motion-collection-catalog-summer-fall-2025/page/1 (footer: (c) 2025 La-Z-Boy Incorporated - 07/2025 - CA0312). Machine-readable copy of everything below: `catalog2025_motion.json` (same folder).

## How this was read (and limits)

- The Publitas per-page URLs (`/page/1`, `/page/2-3`) return only the logo to a scraper: the pages are images drawn client-side. The viewer HTML exposes the publication PDF download link, and that PDF has a text layer. I parsed the PDF with Firecrawl (`parsers: [pdf]`, maxAge 0): **52 PDF pages** (= Publitas pages 1-52; the viewer's meta confirms spread `2-3` = Prefix Definitions).
- The extracted text has **no page-break markers**. Page numbers below are derived from the extraction's section separators and cross-checked against the catalog's own Style Name Index and Table of Contents. **Printed catalog page N = PDF/Publitas page N+10** (inferred; front matter = PDF 1-10, back cover = PDF 52). Treat every PDF page number as inferred; printed page numbers (1-41) are the ones the catalog itself prints.
- Product photos, room shots, line drawings and any text baked into images were not read (text layer only). Some text-layer blocks are out of order or garbled (dealer-logo captions such as `LAZARSON`, `Lane Venture`, `Lazy Boy` are image captions, not content; LaTeX-looking blocks near the pillow table are OCR noise). Select-cover colour names are paired to codes by adjacency in the text and can be mis-paired where the layout interleaves columns; the code lists themselves are reliable. **Pages I could not read as images: all 52 (text layer only, no screenshots taken).** No page was unreadable as text.
- Where a value looks like a printing error, I record it as printed and flag it. Nothing here is invented; items marked *inference* are labelled as such.

## Headline findings

1. **The 2025 catalog is 41 printed product pages covering 30 styles** (512 Pinnacle ... 899 Bennett); 344 style rows (prefix-model) were captured. 247 rows match the app's 2026 motion catalog exactly in dimensions; 35 differ; 15 have no counterpart in the app's motion catalog.
2. **Five 2025 models are not in the app's 2026 motion catalog: Robin 774 (p23), Mateo 775 (p24), Hawthorn 780 (p25), Lennon 787 (p28), Edie 897 (p40).** Mateo, Hawthorn and Edie have no records at all in `style-index-2026.json`; Robin and Lennon have only recliner/motion records there. Four 2026 motion models are new vs 2025: Belmont 804, Baron 807, Dutton 828, Ovation A86 (the 2025 prefix pages carry the callouts "NEW BELMONT in LB192976 - See Recliner Collection catalog for details" and "NEW SHILOH in D207432 - See Stationary Collection catalog for details", i.e. Belmont was then a Recliner-catalog style).
3. **The 2025 prefix pages define 127 prefix codes; 47 are not in the app's `motion-catalog-2026.json` `prefix_definitions`** (stationary sectionals 60A-6SR, sofa/sleep-sofa 510-655, 230/237/240, classics/complements, 390/39U/39X/3CS, 4LP, 90M, P9D, P9E ...). They are listed in A3.
4. **The 2025 Motion catalog does NOT define 44P, 48P, 49P, 4SP, 4QQ, 4VV, 40A, 40B, 40S, 16H, 1HR, 33P, 32P** (or 43P, 41P, 4AP, 4BP, 4DP, 4EP, 04C...). It does define 60A-60R, 6xx, 610, 630, 230/237, 240, 4LP, 90M, 390, 3CS. See A4 for exactly what is and is not defined and the app-side evidence for the rest.
5. Catalog-internal printing errors worth knowing (C3): the La-Z-Motion prefix list swaps 84U and 81U descriptions; several power wall loveseat rows are labelled with the non-power prefix (320-747, 320-786, 320-787, 330-787, 390-787); Palmer 800 armless recliner rows are printed with prefixes 445/U45/X45; Cade 803 U44/U48/U49 rows are shifted.
