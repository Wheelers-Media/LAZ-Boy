## Q1. Does La-Z-Boy have a Canadian web presence, and what Canada-specific content exists?

### Takeaway
Yes. A full Canadian site exists at www.la-z-boy.ca, with its own pages for Customer Care, Parts & Warranty, return policy, financing (`/financing-ca`), free swatches, Performance Fabric Patterns, design services, store locator and product pages (/p/... and /b/... with Canadian URL slugs). Firecrawl could NOT load any la-z-boy.ca page directly (SSL/TLS certificate error, then tunnel failure), so only search-index excerpts are available. Whole-page text of the Canadian warranty, return policy and financing remains unread. https://www.la-z-boy.com/ca is a 404; lazboy.ca (no hyphens) failed with a tunnel error.

### Inferences
- The .ca site appears to run on the same platform and mostly reuse US Customer Care copy (same phone numbers, same policy wording, same performance-fabric list, Synchrony text). The app's note that Canadian warranty differs from the US table cannot be settled from these excerpts; the .ca excerpt only repeats the US "one year upholstery/foam" language. Treat the US-vs-app warranty conflicts as unresolved until the full .ca warranty page is read (e.g. via a tool that can pass the cert error, or dealer PDF).
- Canadian URL slug patterns differ from US (/p/recliners/<slug>/_/R-<code> vs US /p/living-room/recliners/rocking-recliners/<slug>/R-<code>?coverId=). Canada model availability can therefore be checked by searching .ca product slugs; Collage, Harbor Town, Allegra, Brooks, Paxton, Collins confirmed on .ca.

- Whether the .ca site has a Canadian Product Manual index or different PDF links is unknown; product pages do have a "Download Product Manual" link.
- No store list for Canada captured (locator is dynamic).
