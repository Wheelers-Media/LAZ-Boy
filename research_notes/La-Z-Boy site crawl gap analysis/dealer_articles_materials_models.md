## (A) NEW to the app (not found by grep, or app lacks the detail)

### Takeaway
The biggest gaps are: (1) the dealer's fabric-grade *narrative* (grade is cost/design complexity, not durability), the three-level wearability wording and Select vs Custom fabric explanation; (2) the leather finish-type taxonomy (aniline, semi-aniline, nubuck, corrected/pigmented, fully protected) and Crypton leather; (3) iClean and pet-friendly upgrade price ranges, care steps and the stain-coverage list; (4) power-control types with the "Find Me" remote resync procedure and left-side control upcharge; (5) rocker vs glider vs wall vs swivel decision logic; (6) per-model dealer fit guidance, pros/cons and starting prices for Acadia, Quinault, Armitage, Trouper, Collins, Duo and the "tall", "small space", "wall" and "high-leg" shortlists.

### A1. Fabric grades and fabric programs (fabric-grades, 2026-04-19)
- Grades run "B through Q"; each "represents a different balance of price, durability, weave, and texture" — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). (App has bands B-C, D-F, G-N, P-Q in style-index, so the letters are not new; the explanation is.)
- Core message: "Fabric grades are used to categorize upholstery by cost, material, and manufacturing process"; higher grade "reflects how the fabric is made, not how it performs in everyday use"; higher grade is "not necessarily" more durable; "a lower-grade fabric can actually perform better for their needs" — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). App grep for this framing: no hit in the glossary or sales library.
- Dealer-stated factors that set the grade: material type, weave density ("Tighter weaves typically last longer and resist pilling"), construction methods, testing (abrasion, colorfastness, pilling) — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). Also pattern/design and finishes/treatments.
- FAQ: grading systems "vary by manufacturer ... compare fabrics within the same brand rather than across retailers"; "Mid-grade or performance fabrics are often the best choice for high-traffic households" — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades).
- Select vs Custom fabrics: Select Fabrics are "married" to specific frames and included "at no extra charge"; Custom Fabrics are "not tied to a single frame", "do add to the price", "hundreds of possibilities" — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). The model-review articles repeat: "Select fabrics will not affect the starting price of your furniture, but anything beyond that will" — e.g. [review-acadia-furniture-family](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/review-acadia-furniture-family).
- Specialty performance fabrics list (6 families): stain-resistant (iClean), pet-friendly, antimicrobial, eco-friendly (Conserve, "made from recycled water bottles"), energy restoration (Restore by Nanobionic, "mineral-based fabric coating"), bleach cleanable ("diluted bleach") — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). (App already has Conserve, Nanobionic, bleach, antimicrobial entries; list-as-a-set framing is new.)
- Fabric vs leather comparison table (pros and cons, 2 columns) — [fabric-grades](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/fabric-grades). Leather article adds a 6-row table (Durability, Maintenance, Comfort, Style, Cost, Practicality) with "Extremely durable, can last 10–20 years with proper care" vs "Shorter lifespan" — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy). The "10–20 years" claim is a dealer sales statement, not a warranty; do not present as guaranteed life.

### A2. Leather (leather-la-z-boy, 2026-04-25)
- Leather type taxonomy (descriptions quoted from the article): Aniline ("natural, untreated look", "patina"); Semi-Aniline ("light protective coating", "one of the most practical choices"); Nubuck ("Lightly sanded or buffed", "Sensitive to moisture", "lower-traffic areas"); Corrected or Pigmented ("buffed to remove natural imperfections", "Highly resistant to stains, fading, and wear"); Fully Protected ("stronger protective layer", "ideal for high-traffic areas") — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy). App grep: nubuck appears in glossary and fabric-cover-program; semi-aniline only in catalog_data-v2 and sales-reference-library. Aniline/corrected/fully-protected as a customer-facing ladder is not in the glossary. Caution: the dealer maps these to "La-Z-Boy leather" generally; it does not say which cover codes are which finish, so do not map to cover codes without corporate data.
- Three-tier program: Select Leather (specific frames; "Full Leather (LL)" or "Leather Match (LM)"), Custom Leather (most frames, LL and LM), Custom Leather Plus (full top-grain coverage, exclusive textures and colours, higher price) — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy). LL/LM/Custom Leather Plus all exist in the app already (see B).
- Leather Match definition: seats, arms, backs in genuine leather; "sides and back are finished with a closely matched synthetic material" — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy).
- "Leather at La-Z-Boy refers specifically to top-grain leather" — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy).
- Performance leathers: iClean leather ("liquids bead on the surface"), antimicrobial leather, and **Crypton leather** ("resists UV fading, repels stains and dirt, and helps replenish the leather's natural oils") — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy). App grep: "Crypton" appears only in canada_lineup_research.json; not in glossary, mechanism guide or sales library.
- Leather performance "is not measured the same way as fabric. Instead of rub counts, performance depends on how the leather is finished" — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy).
- Leather FAQ: cracking "can occur if leather is not properly maintained"; "Occasional conditioning helps" — [leather-la-z-boy](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/leather-la-z-boy). No step-by-step leather care procedure is on this page (gap).

### A5. Mechanisms and motion chairs
**Rocker vs glider (rocker-recliner-vs.-glider-recliner, 2026-02-04)**
- Rocker: "up to 16 reclining positions"; rocking stops once footrest engaged; "over 70 rocker recliner models"; available as loveseats and sofas. Glider: straight fixed track, "360-degree swivel base" on most, "typically feature one locking footrest position", "around 10 models"; "Glider recliners at La-Z-Boy typically cost $200 to $300 more" — [rocker-recliner-vs.-glider-recliner](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/rocker-recliner-vs.-glider-recliner). The $200-$300 glider premium and model counts are dealer claims; not in app.
- Noise: rockers "clunking or clicking sound" when changing positions is "normal and is part of La-Z-Boy's locking footrest mechanism"; gliders quieter — same page.
- Base construction: rockers "vertical rocker base"; gliders "round swivel base combined with a gliding track" — same page.
- Dealer picks: rockers Pinnacle, Rowan, Trouper; gliders Harbor Town, Sparrow, Finley — same page.

**Swivel vs gliding (swivel-vs.-gliding, 2026-02-07)**
- Swivel: "full 360 degrees", "low, round bases" and "high-leg swivel designs". Glider: "Unlike rocking chairs, which move in an arc, gliders stay level with the floor"; "some gliders can also be equipped with stabilizers". "Wall recliners ... cannot swivel without sacrificing their space-saving function"; "the leg rest must be fully closed for any chair to swivel or glide properly" — [swivel-vs.-gliding](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/swivel-vs.-gliding). (App already says swivel base is not available on wall recliners: see B.)
- Comparison table (Motion type, Best for, Comfort feel, Motion sensitivity, Directional movement, Space) — same page.
- Examples: Pinnacle Gliding Recliner, Reegan Swivel Chair (high-leg, 360), Midtown Swivel Gliding Chair ("compact footprint"), Roxie Swivel Gliding Chair — same page.

**Rocker vs wall (rocker-recliner-vs-wall-recliner, 2026-04-07)**
- Wall recliner = "wall-hugger or wall-away recliner"; slides forward on a track; "hidden metal base and track system"; "Wall recliners typically cost about $70 more" than comparable models; rockers "may feel less stable for seniors"; clearance: rockers "around 12 to 13 inches", wall "only four to five inches" (key takeaways) — [rocker-recliner-vs-wall-recliner](https://www.stylemeetscomfort.ca/learning-center/rocker-recliner-vs-wall-recliner). ## (B) Already in the app (grep-verified)
