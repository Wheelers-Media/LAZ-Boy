# Index gaps

Full detail is in `Index_Gap_Report.xlsx` (Summary, Models, Cap code conflicts, Styles missing from app, Finishes, Dimension conflicts, Option conflicts).

The April 2026 Product Information Guide has, for every style, the same fields as the back of the tag: cap codes, standard and optional finishes, options, backs, seats, pillows, features, and full dimensions. The app has none of the cap codes or finishes per style.

- Cap codes: 63 of 104 models disagree with the guide. 50 show DP/NP/PS/VS the model does not offer, 7 show leather it does not offer, 13 have no cap data.
- Finishes: the app offers 6 finishes on any FN style; the guide gives each style a standard finish and 0 to 3 optional ones.
- Products: Robin 774, Fletcher 424 and 795 are in the guide but not the app; Lakewood 942 and Cuyahoga 958 are in the app but not the guide.
- Options: 20 styles differ; "N/A" appears as an option; Q1 is in neither source.
- Dimensions: 57 style rows differ between catalog and guide.
- Not in the app at all: backs, seats, pillows, features, extra dimensions, new/discontinued.
